JellyJam Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Developer: iteration3

Contact: iteration3games@gmail.com

This policy explains how the JellyJam app ("JellyJam", "the app") handles information. JellyJam is published by iteration3 ("we", "us").

Summary

  • We do not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. JellyJam has no accounts, no analytics and no servers operated by us.
  • Your music server sign-ins (Jellyfin and Plex) stay on your device and are sent only to the servers you choose to connect to.
  • App logs stay on your device. They leave it only if you export them yourself.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some data as described below.

Information stored only on your device

JellyJam keeps the following on your device so it can work. None of it is sent to us.

InformationPurpose
Server addresses, user names and access tokens for the Jellyfin, Plex or DLNA servers you addConnecting to your own music servers. Access tokens are kept in Android's secure, encrypted storage.
Your stations, playlists, favorites, recently played items and a music library indexRunning the radio and library features.
Settings and theme choicesRemembering your preferences.
A diagnostic log of app activity and errorsHelping you report a bug (see "App logs" below).

Android device backup is turned off for JellyJam, so this information is not copied to a cloud backup. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of it.

Signing in to Jellyfin and Plex

  • Jellyfin: You enter your server address, user name and password. They are sent only to the Jellyfin server you entered. JellyJam does not keep your password. It stores only the access token the server returns.
  • Plex: You link JellyJam to your Plex account by entering a short code at plex.tv. You never type your Plex password into JellyJam. The resulting access token is sent only to Plex (plex.tv) and to your Plex servers.
  • DLNA and local music: The app reads music from media servers on your local network, or from audio files on your device that you allow it to access.

We never receive your server addresses, user names, passwords or tokens. The companies or people who run those servers handle information under their own privacy policies.

App logs

JellyJam writes a limited diagnostic log on your device. It records information such as the app version, platform, operating system version, processor architecture and error messages. It is designed to exclude passwords, access tokens and authenticated links.

The log is never uploaded automatically. You can export it only if you choose to, with Save diagnostic log in Settings. You decide where to save it and whether to share it, for example by attaching it to a bug report. If you send us a log, we use it only to investigate your issue.

Similarly, Export data in Settings saves your stations to a file you choose. It does not include passwords or access tokens, and it is never uploaded automatically.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

JellyJam shows ads using Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob software in the app may collect and process:

  • Device and advertising identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information, such as model, operating system and language
  • Ad interactions, such as ad views and taps, and diagnostic information

Google processes this data under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your choices:

  • Where required by law, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, JellyJam asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can review or change your choice at any time with Privacy options in the app's Settings.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in Android settings under Google > Ads or Privacy > Ads, depending on your device.

Children

JellyJam is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data security

Because we do not collect your data, none of it is stored on our systems. On your device, access tokens are kept in Android's encrypted secure storage. JellyJam prefers HTTPS connections and asks for your confirmation before connecting to a server over unencrypted HTTP.

Data retention and deletion

We do not hold any of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete. To remove everything JellyJam stores, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings. To remove JellyJam's access to your Plex account, revoke it at plex.tv under Authorized Devices. For Jellyfin, remove the device in your server's dashboard. For data processed by Google AdMob, see Google's privacy controls at https://myaccount.google.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version here and change the effective date above.
