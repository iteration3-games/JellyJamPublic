What is JellyJam?

Less scrolling. More listening.

Big music libraries are great until you spend more time choosing than listening. JellyJam brings back the simple joy of radio: pick a frequency and something good is already playing. It is built for people who host their own music, and it works just as well with podcasts and internet radio.

Radio mode

Stations that are always on the air

Each station is a shuffled programme made from a source you choose. Like real radio, a station keeps broadcasting while you’re away. Tune back in and you join it mid-song, right where its schedule has reached.

  • A real dial. Drag around the dial or step 0.1 MHz at a time between 87.5 and 108.0.
  • Static between stations. Sound fades through static as you tune, then locks on.
  • Stations from anything. A whole music library, chosen genres or artists, a JellyJam playlist, podcast feeds or a live stream.
  • Album art on the dial. The dial and track panel show what’s on air.
  • Quick setup. First launch offers popular podcasts, SomaFM channels and internet radio, so you can start listening with no server at all.

Library mode

A music player when you want one

Switch to Library to browse and play exactly what you want. The radio stops when Library playback starts, so only one thing ever plays at a time.

  • Browse everything. Artists, albums, genres, songs and your playlists, with an A–Z jump for artists.
  • Search as you type. Artists, albums and songs appear while you type.
  • Pause, resume and seek. Pick up exactly where you left off.
  • Up Next. Preview the next ten songs and jump straight to one.
  • Shuffle your way. Choose what to shuffle and how, including artist-then-song, plus repeat.
  • Favorites and history. Favorites, recently played, recently added and random albums shelves.
  • Lyrics. Time-synced lyrics follow along, and tapping a line jumps to it.
  • Lightweight playlists. Collect songs, albums and artists from any source into one playlist.

Sources

Works with the music you already have

Connect as many sources as you like. Each one can feed radio stations, and music libraries can be browsed separately or all together.

Media Servers

Sign in to either a Jellyfin, Plex, or DLNA media server to access your music remotely.

Google Drive

Stream music stored in your own Google Drive, alongside your other sources.

Podcasts

Search the podcast directory or paste a feed. Episodes air shuffled on their own station.

Internet radio

Find live stations in the Radio Browser directory, add SomaFM channels, or paste a stream address.

Themes

Every song can look different

Mix and match each part of the look, or let JellyJam pick a new combination for every song.

  • Palettes. Over twenty, from Synthwave and Dracula to Nord and Gruvbox, plus Album Artwork, which takes its colors from the cover.
  • Animated track panels. Ocean waves, thunderstorms, confetti, bubbles, arcade sprites and more.
  • Visualizers. Bars, lines, heat, dot matrix and wave styles.
  • Backgrounds. Aurora ribbons, rain, orbiting particles, a cozy dog garden and seasonal scenes.
  • Presets. Halloween, Cyberpunk, Ocean, Kawaii, Hacker, Christmas Lights and more, in one tap.
  • Animations off. One switch turns off decorative motion whenever you prefer.

And also

The little things

Background playback

On Android, keep listening with the screen off and control playback from the notification and lock screen.

Choose your shuffle

Change the shuffle algorithm to suit your mood, such as fully random or artist-then-song.

Live spectrum

An optional visualizer reacts to what’s playing. It’s off until you turn it on.

Move your stations

Export your stations to a file and import them on another device. Passwords and tokens are never included.

Screen reader friendly

Controls are labeled for screen readers, and Back or Escape closes the top popup first.

Bug reports on your terms

Save a diagnostic log only if you want to attach it to a bug report. It’s never sent automatically.
